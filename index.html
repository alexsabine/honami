Approach

How I work

Teaching is the skill I bring to every piece of work: workshops, plain-language guides, and training non-technical staff until they can do the work without me.

Good change is grown, not forced.

Rhythm

Integration and action

A change depends less on its size than on its timing. I help you find a pace that is neither thrash nor lock-in.

Form

Aesthetic compression

Beauty is not decoration. It is how meaning travels quickly and is remembered. The work should be hospitable and precise at once.

Lightness

Playful by design

New technology tends to make organisations rigid. Workshops bring hard ideas to life through play, so a team can pick them up and handle them.

Continuity

Gracefully adaptive

To adapt well is to bend freely while something at the centre stays whole. New tools arrive at a pace your people and values can absorb.

About

A teacher first

I have taught at every phase from early years to higher education, designed whole PGCE programmes, and founded an early years nursery.

Until 2025 I was at the University of Portsmouth: Principal Lecturer for Enterprise and Innovation in the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences, and Senior Teaching Fellow in Childhood Studies from 2017.

It began in English literature and psychology, and arrived, by way of a doctorate in participatory mapping, at the science of how adaptive systems hold together and change. The instinct has always been to make hard things legible.

Now I work for myself. Honami is a technology and education consultancy for research, arts and community organisations in the UK and California.

A capable mind, given a rich environment and unhurried attention, will largely teach itself.
